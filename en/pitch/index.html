Rise Above CreatorsA proposal for The Climbing Corner
NL View the prototype
For Adam and Nika · October 2026

Your climbers
already
share it.

The gym is full of creative people who film, post and share their sends. Rise Above Creators gives that creativity a home. Climbers make videos in their own way and earn climbing credit for it. The gym gets a lot of eyes on the content and, if it works well, new people on the mats.

Why

Ads
feel like
ads

A close-knit communityAnyone who walks into The Climbing Corner notices it straight away: people know each other, help each other on the mats and love the place. That enthusiasm is the best thing there is to share.
Almost half are newYour own website says it: about 50% of new visitors have never climbed before. Those people need to see that it is for them too. A climber from Heerlen shows that better than any ad.
People already filmClimbers film their attempts and their sends. This programme gives them visuals in your style, their own code and a reward of credits. What they make with it is up to them.
How it works

Play. Post.
Earn.

01

Win something

Creators play short games about bouldering. That wins them motion videos in your house style and other things for their own Reels.

02

Post with a code

Every creator gets their own code on an end card: "First time? Mention LOTTE at reception."

03

Earn climbing credit

Points for checked views and above all for new climbers. Trade them for entries, shoe rental, a session or a month of membership.

Everyone wins

Good for
the creators,
good for the gym

For the creatorsA reward of credits, and room to express themselves. They make videos their own way, with visuals they unlocked themselves.
For the gymA lot of eyes on the content, from people who know and trust the creators. That puts the gym in view and can draw new people to the mats.
That keeps it turningCreators play, make videos, earn credit and come back. The game is one way to keep that going. There are more, and we would love to think them up together.

What it means for you

Money that
stays in the gym

With ads, the budget goes to a big platform somewhere far away. Here it goes to the people who are already in the gym. To me, that is the best thing about this idea.

With ads
  • The budget goes to a big platform
  • You also pay for viewers far outside Parkstad
  • People see an ad and scroll on
Rise Above Creators
  • The budget goes to your own climbers as climbing credit
  • Videos by real people from the gym, in their own style
  • Creators climb more often, often bringing a friend
  • Lots of eyes on the gym, and hopefully new faces on the mats
In control of the costs

You hold
the reins

Maximum per creatorNobody earns more than 600 points a month (€60 in credit). You set the maximum and all the amounts yourselves.
Everything is checkedCreators send in their link and a screenshot of their views. Points are only added after a check.
Ten seconds at receptionSomeone mentions a code? Type it into the admin screen. That is all the work on your side.
What I build

Everything you see
in the prototype

The site

A dedicated subsite, for example crew.theclimbingcorner.com, in the gym's house style. Works on phones.

Games

Five to start with, and up to twenty are possible. I build the games that fit your gym and what creators enjoy.

A growing vault

To start, eleven motion visuals, including the end card with a code. I will add more, and other things to win too. How many, we can discuss together.

Dashboard for creators

Code, points, submissions, leaderboard and rewards. Real accounts instead of storage in the browser.

Admin for the gym

Reviewing, registering codes at reception and a monthly overview of views, new climbers and credit given out.

Launch

An announcement on Instagram, a poster at reception and a kick-off evening with the first creators.
